Prenatal Home Services

Convenient prenatal support from a qualified midwife in the comfort of your home.

What can be included
  • Maternal vital signs and weight monitoring
  • Pregnancy history, concerns, and basic prenatal assessment
  • Fetal heart rate and fundic height monitoring
  • Basic prenatal monitoring and referral coordination when indicated
  • Nutrition, healthy lifestyle, iron and folic acid education
  • Breastfeeding preparation and positioning/attachment education
  • Birth planning, hospital preparation, and mother-and-baby essentials checklist
  • Pregnancy danger-sign education and guidance on when to seek immediate medical care
♡

First-Time Mother Care

Extra guidance for mothers experiencing pregnancy and motherhood for the first time.

  • Pregnancy, labor, and birth preparation
  • Birth plan assistance
  • Newborn and breastfeeding education
  • Hygiene, self-care, and nutrition guidance
  • Mother-and-baby preparation checklist
⌂

Postpartum Home Care

Continued support after birth for maternal recovery, breastfeeding, and newborn care.

  • Maternal vital signs and postpartum assessment
  • Uterine involution and lochia education
  • Breastfeeding and breast care support
  • Newborn care, nutrition, hygiene, and self-care guidance
  • Postpartum danger-sign education and referral when needed
+

Maternal Immunization Service

Tetanus protection at home may be provided when appropriate and within the midwife's authorized scope and applicable local requirements.

  1. Review immunization history
  2. Assess whether vaccination is indicated
  3. Explain the vaccine and expected reactions
  4. Obtain appropriate consent
  5. Administer according to applicable guidelines
  6. Document vaccination
  7. Provide post-vaccination monitoring instructions
Pregnancy danger signs need prompt medical attention. HerCare education includes awareness of concerns such as vaginal bleeding, severe headache or visual disturbance, severe abdominal pain, swelling or other concerning symptoms, and decreased fetal movement. Seek immediate medical care when advised or when symptoms are urgent.

ASK HERCARE

Questions? Ask our midwives.

Mothers can send questions before or after a home visit. Choose a topic, share your question, and HerCare can use it to guide follow-up support.

Example

Mother: “I'm 28 weeks pregnant and I'm a working mom. What should I prepare before going to the hospital?”

HerCare: “We can help you prepare a birth plan and hospital bag checklist during your home visit. If you have concerning symptoms such as bleeding, severe abdominal pain, severe headache, or decreased fetal movement, seek immediate medical care.”

The question is emailed to the HER CARE administrator. The client receives an SMS acknowledgement when SMS is configured, with email used as the fallback.

For urgent symptoms or emergencies, seek immediate medical care rather than waiting for a response through this form.
